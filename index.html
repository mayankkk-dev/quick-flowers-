Our Story

A neighborhood shop, tied by hand.

LA Quick Flowers started as a single flower stand and grew into a full studio because of one idea: bouquets should feel personal, not mass-produced. Every arrangement leaving our shop is hand-tied the same day it's ordered.

Alongside our everyday bouquets, our lead designer creates a rotating set of signature masterpieces — small-batch, statement arrangements you won't find anywhere else in the city.
